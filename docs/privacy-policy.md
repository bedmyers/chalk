# Chalk Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 26, 2026_

Chalk is a weightlifting log that tells you what to lift and adds weight when you earn it. It's built to keep your data yours.

## What Chalk stores

Chalk stores the programs you set up and the workouts you log: exercise names, sets, reps, and weights, plus your preferred unit.

## Where it's stored

- **On your device**, in the app's private storage.
- **In your own iCloud account**, in Chalk's private iCloud database, so your data syncs across your devices. This is part of your iCloud account and governed by [Apple's privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). The developer can't see or access it.

## What Chalk doesn't do

- No accounts or sign-in.
- No analytics, advertising, or tracking.
- No data is collected by, sent to, or shared with the developer or any third party.

## Deleting your data

Deleting a program in Chalk deletes it and its workouts from your device and your iCloud account. Deleting the app removes the on-device copy. To remove the iCloud copy as well, go to Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Storage → Chalk and delete its data.

## Children

Chalk isn't directed at children under 13 and doesn't knowingly collect any information from anyone.
